Heritage Site Plan

Site Name: Heritage

Heritage is the name of a video game I desire to create. I chose the name because I envisioned players being able to play as descendants of characters they won the game with.

Site Purpose

The website will be used to raise awareness of Heritage. A blog will provide ongoing information about development, while a history page will explain the background and motivation of making Heritage. Users will also have the option to register, which will give them access to a secret page.

Scenario Questions

  • What kind of game will Heritage be?
  • Why should I get this game?
  • Are there any red flags for Heritage?

Color Schema

  • Dark Green: Background
  • Pale Goldenrod: Headings
  • White: Regular Text
